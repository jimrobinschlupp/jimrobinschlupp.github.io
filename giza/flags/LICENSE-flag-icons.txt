The SVG files in this folder (de.svg, dk.svg, eg.svg, es.svg, fr.svg, gb.svg,
gr.svg, iq.svg, it.svg, jp.svg, us.svg) are the 4x3 country flags copied
unmodified from the flag-icons project:

  https://github.com/lipis/flag-icons

Only the country codes this app's data actually uses were copied; the rest of
that repository (its build tooling, CSS, and other flags) was not vendored.
