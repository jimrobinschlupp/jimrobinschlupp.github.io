Take it with you

The icon, the trip and everyone on it are yours. Running it for real means putting your own copy somewhere, and that somewhere is a free Vercel account.

  1. Copy the app. The button below hands it to Vercel, who put their own copy in your account along with the storage it needs. You pick two PINs on the way: one the group gets, one just for you.
  2. Sign in and let it build. A free account, a couple of minutes, and the app is at an address only you know.
  3. Bring this trip over. Copy the trip code, open your app, unlock it with your own PIN, and paste. The dates, the people and the flights on this page arrive in one go.
  4. Add an AI key if you want one. Optional, and the only part that can ever cost money. Everything else works without it.

Free to run. The trip code never leaves this browser until you paste it, and it is not a secret once it does: it is everything on this page, readable by anyone holding it. It belongs in your own app and nowhere else.
